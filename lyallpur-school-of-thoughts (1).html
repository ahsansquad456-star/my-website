ABOUT US

Named for a city that built itself on trade, thought and craft.

Lyallpur — the name Faisalabad carried for a century — grew as a city of grain markets, textile mills and grid-planned bazaars. We borrow that name deliberately: a reminder that this city has always rewarded people who think carefully and work with their hands as well as their heads.

Our classrooms follow the national curriculum in full, taught in English, with smaller sections so teachers can actually know each student's progress rather than guess at it.

Small class sizes

No section larger than what one teacher can genuinely track.

English-medium instruction

Built around the national curriculum, taught with local context.

Character alongside grades

Weekly assemblies, debate and community service built into the week.

ACADEMICS

Three stages, one continuous standard.

Every stage is taught by teachers who specialise in that age group, with a shared expectation of discipline and curiosity across all three.

NURSERY – KG

Foundation Years

Phonics, numeracy and guided play, in classrooms sized for four- and five-year-olds, not scaled down from older grades.

GRADE 1 – 8

Middle School

Full national curriculum in English, Urdu, Mathematics, Science and Islamiyat, with weekly labs from Grade 4 onward.

GRADE 9 – MATRIC

Secondary School

Science and Humanities groups, board-exam preparation, and subject teachers who stay with their students through both years.
